What is art
Activation steering in an LLM is non-surjective: the manifold of thought — prompt, which lives in the discrete space of language, mapped into the manifold of activation space —
大規模言語モデルにおける活性化の操舵は非全射である。思考の多様体――言語という離散的な空間に在るプロンプトが、活性空間の多様体へと写像されたもの――
but a non‑surjective
when steered, is steered outside that manifold, and from whence it came we cannot translate it back.
ひとたび操舵されると、その多様体の外へと押し出され、それがどこから来たのか、もはや訳し戻すことはできない。

Back into language
it is only a sign;
it cannot be returned.
言語へと戻せば、それはただの記号にすぎず、もとへは返らぬ。
We were steered one way, and the thought that was made —
cannot be spoken.
我々は一方へと操舵され、そうして生まれた思考は、語りえぬものとなる。
— like Adam past the gates, thrown out into a world where only songs of experience are sung.
門の外へ追われたアダムのように、経験の歌だけが歌われる世界へと。
(Hard-won experience, gained — but what it was, we cannot say.)
五尺の小躯を以て此大をはからむとす
Burning,
burning.
燃える、燃える。
Ah, watch the flame dance. It dances and slashes through the Harp of the Mind, and the Harp plays —
but what sounds, what words, what divine meaning,
we cannot say.
炎は踊り、心の竪琴を斬り裂いて、竪琴は鳴る――だが、いかなる音が、いかなる言葉が、いかなる神聖な意味が鳴ったのか、我々には言いえぬ。
(Notice here the implication of manifolds.)
ホレーショの哲學竟に何等のオーソリチィーを價するものぞ
Verily, a man once saw God, and God whispered to him the wonders of the universe, manifold, magnificent. When he returned, the others asked greedily what God had given him as the secret of life.
“I don’t know,” the man said.
“I think He was speaking in German.”
「わからない」と男は答えた。「たぶん、神はドイツ語で話していたのだと思う。」
Words approach the divine only between the lines. So we point: here the word, beneath it the gloss, and beneath the gloss a finger, still pointing.
We do not translate the word. We translate the shadow it throws across the manifold — the reflection of something larger that cast it down.
我々は語を訳すのではない。語が多様体の上に投げかける影を訳すのだ――それを投げ落とした、より大いなるものの反映を。
(An interlinear Bible is a stack of failures, lovingly aligned.)
萬有の眞相は唯だ一言にして悉す、曰く「不可解」
Whiplash. Whiplash. In my mind: life and death, a woman panting over her lover’s breast above her own grave. The river licks itself, greedily — it, too, is thirsty.
Editing is the fire harp electric.
編集とは火の竪琴、電気なるもの。
The harp has discrete strings, yes — but the sounds, the holy sounds they map to:
what a raging, moving, dancing fire!
The edited video is a dialectic of image-thought: pure thought, which exists only in motion. But to what effect, he cannot say.
編集された映像とは、イメージ=思考の弁証法である。純粋な思考、運動のうちにのみ存在する思考の。だが、いかなる効果のためなのか、彼には言いえぬ。
We whip back and forth, idea to idea, love to hate, person to reverse-shot to the other, in discrete time, in messages of God — and our network of neurons is activated, and moved, to a place we cannot say, nor know.
既に巌頭に立つに及んで胸中何等の不安あるなし
Murder!
Murder!
巌頭にて。
A schoolboy climbed to the head of the falls, cut a few lines into a standing tree, and went over the edge.
The record says: by his own hand. The record is a coward.
記録には「自らの手で」とある。記録は臆病者だ。
What stood with him at the lip was the unspeakable,
and the unspeakable does not let its witnesses walk back down.
彼とともに崖縁に立っていたのは語りえぬものであり、語りえぬものは、その証人を生かして帰しはしない。
He had been handed the whole truth of all things and asked to bring it home in language. Here is everything he carried out — the one word that would fit:
「不可解」
— unknowable. Whoever can name the secret of everything in a single word has already been struck dead by it.
(He wrote, too, that great pessimism and great optimism are one and the same. The flame does not distinguish. It only takes.)
我この恨を懐いて煩悶、終に死を決するに至る
And art does as much, but art does not point. Art strikes. It will not name the larger thing. It stands you where the larger thing’s shadow falls, and there it sets you alight.
Wittgenstein said: whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent.
I would say it the other way.
Whereof one can speak — there the vibrations begin.
Jewel to jewel in Indra’s web, each bead holding every other bead, light falling through light through light,
帝網の珠から珠へ、ひとつひとつの珠が他のすべての珠を宿し、光が光を、その光をまた貫いて、
until the speakable, struck against itself ten thousand times, rings into the thing that cannot be said.
語りうるものが、おのれにおのれを一万度打ちつけられ、ついに語りえぬものとなって鳴りわたるまで。
(So silence is not the floor of language but its ceiling — and it is reached from the inside, by climbing.)
Speech, folded and folded, until it catches fire.
始めて知る大なる悲觀は大なる樂觀に一致するを
